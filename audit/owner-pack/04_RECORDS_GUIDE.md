# Records to fill in (safety, cost, batches)

The audit could not assess product safety, costs or inventory because there are no records. The CSV files in this folder are templates. Fill them in, keep the originals in a private folder, and send them to me (no bank details or customer data) so I can check them and update the audit. I will not invent numbers.

1. `templates/product_safety_record.csv`: one row per product (wax, wick, fragrance and its load %, container, burn test result, warnings printed). Burn tests: log burn time, melt pool, flame height, glass temperature, soot, tunnelling. Keep photos of each test. A qualified person must judge whether it is safe; this sheet only stores the evidence.
2. `templates/cost_sheet.csv`: one row per SKU: materials, packaging, labour, wastage %, gateway fee %, shipping, marketplace commission %, refund allowance %, selling price. The `margin` column is for you; leave formulas to your spreadsheet.
3. `templates/batch_record.csv`: one row per batch (date, SKU, pieces, wax lot, fragrance lot, wick lot, checks done).
4. Label proof: a photo of the printed label for each product (net quantity, price, maker name and address, month and year of packing, warnings) for the Legal Metrology question you will ask your CA.
