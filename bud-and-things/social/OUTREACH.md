# Outreach and reply scripts

Replace `[name]`, `[handle]` and `[city]` yourself. Send one by one, to people you actually know or have really looked at. **Never mass-message strangers with a sales line (spam).** Never include a phone number, address, building or society name. Say "By Ashi" or "Ashi" only.

**Do not use society or RWA WhatsApp groups until the RWA has replied to the licence enquiry.**

---

## 1 · Personal message to friends and family (WhatsApp, 30 people over 2 days)

### OUT-1
```text
Hi [name]! A small thing I've been working on: I've started BUD & THINGS, hand-poured candles and Jesmonite gifts, made by me, from ₹15. 🕯️

Would you follow the page and tell me which one you like best? It helps me a lot: [handle]

The shop opens soon, you can join the list at budandthings.com. Thank you 🤍
```
*Why it works:* it asks for one small thing, not a purchase. After the shop is open, send a second short message to the same people with the link.

## 2 · Pitch to a nano creator (Instagram DM, 3 to 5 a week)

Before you send: follow them for a week, like and comment on three real posts, and read what they usually post.

### OUT-2
```text
Hi [name], I really liked your post about [specific post]. I'm Ashi, I make hand-poured candles and Jesmonite trays in Greater Noida (BUD & THINGS). 🕯️

I'd love to send you one piece as a gift, with no obligation. If you like it and want to share, I'd only ask that you label it as gifted. If it isn't right for you, no problem at all.

May I send you a few options?
```
**Rules:** wait for a yes before sending anything. Send the piece to the address they give you; do not store or post it. Ask them to label the post (#gifted, or Instagram's Paid partnership label). Never ask for a positive review, only an honest one. Do not offer money for a good review.

## 3 · Local small business (cross-promotion)

### OUT-3
```text
Hi [name], I love [specific thing]. I'm Ashi from BUD & THINGS, a small handmade candle and Jesmonite shop in Greater Noida. 🤍

Would you like to do a small swap: you share a photo of my [candle or dish] with your pieces, and I share yours with mine? We both reach new people, and it costs nothing.
```

## 4 · Office or HR: Secret Santa and team gifts

### OUT-4
```text
Hi [name], I'm Ashi from BUD & THINGS, hand-poured candles and Jesmonite gifts from ₹15, wrapped with a hand-written note. 🎁

For Secret Santa or team gifts, I can put together a mix at a budget per person, such as ₹100, ₹200 or ₹300. I make everything by hand in small batches, so I need the number of gifts and the date a few weeks ahead.

Would you like me to suggest a set for your team? I'll send options and prices first.
```
Only agree to a quantity you can make. Confirm lead time honestly.

```text
Hi [name], I'm Ashi from BUD & THINGS, a small handmade gift shop in Greater Noida. 🤍

For fests, Secret Santa or farewell gifts, we have candles and Jesmonite pieces from ₹15, wrapped with a note. May I share the link with your group, or send a small price list for your members?
```

## 6 · Saved replies (Instagram and WhatsApp)

Save these as quick replies. Always check the numbers against the site.

### REPLY-price
```text
Thank you for asking! 🤍 Prices start at ₹15 for a tealight, and go up to ₹299. You can see every price at budandthings.com (link in bio).
```
### REPLY-delivery
```text
Delivery takes 4 to 7 days. The shipping fee is shown at checkout, and the shipping page on the site lists where we deliver. You can track your order on the site.
```
### REPLY-soldout
```text
Thank you for asking! That one is sold out for now. Everything is made in small batches. You can ask the site to tell you when it's back (on the product page), and I'll let you know.
```
### REPLY-custom
```text
Thank you! I'd love to hear what you have in mind. Please tell me what you'd like, the number you need, and when. Everything is made by hand, so I'll tell you honestly what is possible and how long it takes.
```
### REPLY-bulk
```text
Thank you for thinking of us! For team or event gifts, tell me the number of gifts, your budget per person and the date. I'll suggest a set and a price before you decide.
```
### REPLY-order
```text
You can order at budandthings.com (link in bio). Add your gift note and wrap at checkout. If anything is unclear, message me and I'll help. 🤍
```
Do not give a personal phone number in any reply.

## 7 · Thank-you and review request (after delivery)

### OUT-7
```text
Hi [name], I hope your [product] arrived safely. 🤍 Thank you so much for ordering from BUD & THINGS.

If you'd like to, I'd love to hear what you thought, good or not so good, and a photo if you're happy to share one. May I share it on my page? (I'll only post it if you say yes, and I'll tag or name you only if you want me to.)
```
**Rules:** an honest review only. Don't offer a discount or gift in return for a *positive* review. Only post what the person has actually said, unedited, with a yes (a DM is fine). Keep the screenshot in a folder as proof of permission.
