# BUD & THINGS by Ashi: social media strategy

Launch: **Fri 20 Nov 2026**. This plan covers 14 Nov to 31 Dec 2026. Written 17 Nov, so 14 to 16 Nov have already passed (see CALENDAR.md).

## 1. The one-line idea

*Made with thought. Given with feeling.* (the site's own line). Small-batch candles and Jesmonite objects, made by hand by Ashi in Greater Noida, that are easy to give.

Social media has one job in the first six weeks: **get people to see how the pieces are made, then give one to someone.** Everything below serves that.

## 2. Audience

| Who | What they want | Where they are |
|---|---|---|
| **Gift-givers, women 22 to 40, Delhi NCR** (Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Gurugram, Delhi). Main audience | A gift under ₹300 that looks thought-through: friends, colleagues, in-laws, Secret Santa, housewarming | Instagram, Pinterest, WhatsApp |
| **Self-treaters, 20 to 35** | A pretty candle or trinket dish for their own desk or shelf | Instagram Reels, Shorts |
| **Students and first-job crowd** (Greater Noida has several universities) | Tiny gifts at ₹15 to ₹99 | Instagram, Shorts |
| **Office and small-business gifters** | Many small gifts at once, wrapped | WhatsApp, Instagram DMs |

The price ladder is the strength: ₹15 tealight, ₹29 ladoo candle, ₹79 dish, ₹99 jar, ₹149 tray, ₹169 to ₹299 glass and Murphy candles. Show it. Prices are part of the content (see "Prices" below).

## 3. Four content pillars

Rough weekly mix: 3 making, 2 gifting, 1 festive/occasion, 1 customer love. Pillar tag in CALENDAR.md: **M**, **G**, **F**, **C**.

1. **M · Making process.** Pouring, colouring, scenting, moulding Jesmonite, trimming wicks, packing. This is the content nobody else can copy and it costs nothing extra: film what you are already doing. Hands, wax, steam, satisfying close-ups.
2. **G · Gifting moments.** Who the gift is for and when: Secret Santa, a friend's housewarming, the colleague who covered for you. Budget tiers, wrapping, the hand-written note, "what to give someone who has everything".
3. **F · Festive and occasion edits.** Kartik Purnima and Gurpurab (24 Nov), Christmas, winter solstice, New Year's Eve, wedding-season host gifts. Light, warm, never pushy.
4. **C · Customer love.** Real parcels, real messages, real photos from real customers, with their permission. **Until there are some, this pillar runs on genuine two-way things:** polls, "ask Ashi" answers, DMs you actually received. Every C slot in the calendar has a fallback post so nothing fake ever fills the gap.

## 4. Tone of voice

Warm, specific, a little playful. A friend who makes things, not a brand shouting.

- **Say:** "I poured this one on Sunday." "Smells like a coffee shop." "A ₹15 way to say thank you." Short sentences. One idea per post. First person ("I") for making posts, because the brand is Ashi's.
- **Hinglish: light.** At most one Hinglish line or phrase per caption, where it comes naturally ("ek chhota sa gift", "mithai wali khushboo", "diwali ke baad bhi roshni"). Roman script. Never forced, never a whole caption.
- **Don't say:** "luxury", "premium", "best", "No.1", "pure", "natural", "organic", "non-toxic", "eco-friendly", "toxin-free", "stress-relief", "therapy", "aromatherapy", anything with a burn-time number. We have not tested or certified those claims, and several need proof under Indian advertising rules. If Ashi later has proof (a wax supplier's certificate, a measured burn test), it can be added with the evidence kept on file.
- **Say what is true and plain:** hand-poured, hand-moulded, small batch, made in Greater Noida, scent names as listed on the site.

## 5. Honest marketing rules (ASCI and consumer law)

These are rules for this plan, not suggestions. The relevant framework in India: the Consumer Protection Act 2019, the CCPA's guidelines on misleading advertisements and endorsements (2022), its dark-pattern guidelines (2023), the Department of Consumer Affairs' endorsement guidance for influencers (2023) and ASCI's code and influencer guidelines. Read the current texts yourself; they are updated.

1. **Stock is real and tiny.** Most pieces have 1 to 6 units. Post only what the Owner view shows as in stock *today*. Never imply a piece is available when it is at 0.
2. **No fake urgency.** No countdown clocks that reset, no "only 2 left!" unless it is true at the moment of posting, no "last chance" unless it is the last chance. "Small batch" is true; "hurry" is not needed.
3. **Real prices.** Prices come from the site. Mention shipping honestly: ₹99, free over ₹1,999 (check the Owner view settings before posting).
4. **No invented reviews or testimonials.** Customer-love posts use real messages, unedited, with the sender's permission, and the order has actually been delivered. No "customers love us" without customers.
5. **Offers only if live.** The welcome code (HELLO10, 10% off first order) may be mentioned only while the Owner view shows it active. Captions keep it in a separate "offer line" you delete if it is not live.
6. **Photos must show what ships.** The site's current catalogue pictures are marked *temporary* in the README. Posting them as if they were photographs of the actual piece could mislead. Use your own photos (put them in `photos/`). The exporter flags temporary images and `--final` refuses to export them.
7. **Gifted products and collabs are labelled.** If you send a free product to anyone who posts about it, they must label it (#gifted / #ad / Instagram's Paid partnership label), and you should check they do. Your own posts about your own shop are not "ads" in this sense, but if you pay for a boost, Instagram labels it.
8. **No health, safety or performance claims you cannot back.** The only care claims in this plan come from the site's own care text: let the first burn reach the edges (about 2 hours), trim the wick to 5 mm, never leave a burning candle unattended. Add: keep away from children, pets and anything flammable.
9. **Ladoo candles look edible.** Always say "not for eating, only for lighting" on any post that shows one.
10. **Privacy.** No personal phone numbers, home address or private contact details in any post, caption or graphic. Orders go through the website; DMs through Instagram. Customers' names and photos only with their written (DM) permission.
11. **No competitor jabs, no copied music or images.** Use Instagram's royalty-free audio (a business account can only use that library), and your own photos.

## 6. Platform split

| Platform | Role | Cadence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** (main) | Discovery and trust: Reels, carousels, Stories | 6 feed posts a week (2 to 3 Reels, 2 carousels, 1 to 2 single-image) and Stories every day | Link in bio to the site. Add the site link sticker on product Stories. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Same Reels, longer shelf life in search | 3 a week, the same day as the Reel (no new filming) | Upload the clean video (no Instagram watermark). Use the title and description from `captions/youtube-pinterest.md`. |
| **WhatsApp Status** | Warm audience: friends, family, early customers | Daily, 1 image or 15-second clip | The Stories template images work as they are. Status only reaches people who saved the shop's number, so say "save this number" once at launch. |
| **WhatsApp Channel** | Broadcast: new pours, restocks, gift cards | 2 a week (Tue, Fri) | Short. Only news that is true that day. |
| **Pinterest** | Slow-burning search: "Christmas gift ideas India", "Jesmonite tray" | Auto-feed already built (products.xml) plus 5 hand-made pins a week, batched on Sundays | The feed publishes live products only. Hand pins use the carousel images and link to product pages. |

Facebook, X and LinkedIn are skipped for now. Revisit in January.

## 7. Weekly rhythm (a normal week)

| Day | Instagram feed | Stories / Status |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Carousel (gifting or care) | Poll or question |
| Tue | Reel (making) | Behind the scenes |
| Wed | Single image or Reel | Product Story with link |
| Thu | Carousel or Reel | Packing or a customer moment if real |
| Fri | Product or festive post | Stock update (only if true) |
| Sat | Reel | Light, personal |
| Sun | Rest | Q&A, plan the week, batch Pins |

Launch week (20 to 26 Nov) posts every day.

## 8. Measuring (keep it simple)

Look once a week at: saves and shares (these matter more than likes), profile visits, link taps, and orders that mention Instagram. Write one line in a notebook: "what got saved most?" and make more of that.

## 9. Open questions for the owner

- **Instagram handle.** The site footer links to `@budandthings_offical`. Is that spelling right? (It lives in `brand.json`; change once there.)
- **Prices on graphics.** Templates show prices by default. Set `"showPrices": false` in `brand.json` to hide them.
- **Product names.** The catalogue says "Angle & Amber" (probably "Angel & Amber"?) and "Murphy candle" (is this the right trade name for the gel-style jars?). Plans use the catalogue's names as they are. Fix them in the Owner view first, then the content follows.
- **Christmas delivery dates.** The plan deliberately has no order-by or last-dispatch date. Posts say "order early" and quote the site's delivery time (4 to 7 days). Add a date later only after checking real courier times.
- **Gift boxes.** The site has gift-box wording but the current catalogue has no gift-box product. The unboxing Reel shows a gift-wrapped order (₹99 wrap, hand-written note), not a boxed set, until a box exists.
