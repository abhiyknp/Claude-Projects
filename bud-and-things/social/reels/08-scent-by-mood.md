# 08 · Pick a scent by mood

**Post:** Wed 9 Dec · **Length:** 25 s · **Caption:** `1209-IG` · **Shorts:** `1209-YT`

**Hook (0 to 2 s):** Three candles in a row, one lit. Text: **"What's your mood tonight?"**

**CTA:** "Comment your number, I'll tell you which one." Then answer every comment.

**Honesty checks:** "Moods" are fun matches, not therapy. Do not say a scent "relieves", "calms" or "heals". Check each candle is in stock before showing it; do not feature any at 0.
