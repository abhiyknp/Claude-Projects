## Rules for every Reel

- **Hook in the first 2 seconds:** a striking visual plus 4 to 6 words of on-screen text, at once. No logo intro, no "hi guys".
- **On-screen text** stays inside the middle of the frame (away from the top 250 px and bottom 340 px, where Instagram's own buttons sit). Montserrat or Playfair Display, cream or gold, a dark maroon strip behind if the background is bright.
- **Audio:** pick from Instagram's own royalty-free library (a business account cannot use popular commercial songs). The scripts describe a mood, not a song. For Shorts, use YouTube's Audio Library or the same original sound.
- **Captions:** turn on auto-captions or type them in; many people watch muted.
- **Film what is true.** Where a script says "film what you actually do", the steps listed are a guide. If your method differs, show your method.
- **Check stock** for any product named on screen on the day you post.
- Cover frame: use a clean product still with the hook text, in the 4:5 safe area so the profile grid crops well.
