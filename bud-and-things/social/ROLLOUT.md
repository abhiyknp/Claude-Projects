## The daily 20 minutes that grow the account

This is where followers come from at the start. It matters more than the posts.

| Min | Do | How |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Reply** to every comment and DM | Within the hour if you can. Ask a question back so it becomes a conversation |
| 10 | **Comment on 10 posts** from the accounts below | At least 8 words, specific to the post ("the colour on that tray is lovely, is it a marbled pour?"). No emoji-only, no links, no "check my page" |
| 3 | One **Story** with a sticker | Poll, quiz, question box, or "add yours". Stories build the habit |
| 2 | **Save** two Reels for ideas | Instagram then shows you more of the same |

### Who to follow and talk to (150 accounts over the first 3 weeks)

Follow with a reason, 10 to 20 a day. Don't use follow-unfollow or buy followers.

| Group | Examples to search | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Nano creators in NCR** (1k to 10k) | home decor, gifting, "Noida/Delhi NCR lifestyle", student lifestyle | Most likely to collaborate, and their followers are your buyers |
| **Local small businesses** | bakers, jewellery makers, stationery, plant sellers in Noida and Greater Noida | Cross-promotion; you share the same audience |
| **Candle, Jesmonite and resin makers across India** | search `#jesmonite`, `#handpouredcandles` | Learn what they post, and be visible to their followers |
| **Gifting accounts and gift-guide writers** | "gift ideas India", "Secret Santa" | They write the lists you want to be on |
| **Your own contacts** | friends, family, colleagues | The first 30 to 50 followers |

Also study 10 established candle brands for 15 minutes each (for example Pinenlime, Umbrtree and Rad Living, named in a YourStory feature). Note: what they post most, which Reels got more comments, what they never do. Copy the *method*, never the pictures or words.

## How followers turn into customers

```
Reel or Story → profile → bio + highlights → link → site → order
                                          ↘ DM / WhatsApp question → link
Waitlist email → welcome code → first order
```

Make each step easy:
1. **Every post** ends with one clear action ("link in bio", "tell me below", "send this to your Secret Santa").
2. **Product Stories** carry the link sticker to the exact product page.
3. **Answer DMs with the link.** Use the saved replies in `OUTREACH.md`.
4. **Keep the first-buy offer simple.** HELLO10 (10% off) only while live in the Owner view. Do not add more discounts; the price ladder is already low.
5. **After the first deliveries,** ask each customer for a photo and a line, with a yes. That becomes your first real Customer love post.

## Weekly review (Sunday, 10 minutes)

Look at the last 7 days in Instagram Insights and write one line for each:

1. **Which post got the most saves and sends?** Make three more like it.
2. **How many profile visits, link taps and waitlist signups?** (Profile visits → link taps → orders is your funnel.)
3. **Which Story sticker got the most answers?**
4. **How many DMs became orders?**
5. **What is in stock, and what should I pour next?** Pour first, post second.

## "0 to 100": what to aim for (targets, not forecasts)

I can't predict follower numbers and don't want to invent them. These are *working targets* for the first month, with the main source of each:

| Milestone | Where most of it comes from |
|---|---|
| **First 100 real followers** | About 50 from your own contacts (messages and Stories), about 25 from daily commenting on local accounts, about 25 from Reels |
| **First 10 orders** | Warm network and waitlist, not strangers |
| **First 3 real reviews or photos** | Customers you ask personally, with permission |
| **First creator collaboration** | 3 to 5 polite pitches a week for 3 weeks |
| **First 250 followers** | Collaborations and the Christmas Reels |

If a milestone is missed, it is information, not failure: look at the weekly review and change one thing.

## Rules to keep in mind every day

- Real stock only; no fake urgency. Real photos only. No health, burn-time or "natural" claims.
- No address, no phone number, no building or society name, no personal legal name.
- **Do not post the shop in society or RWA WhatsApp groups** until the RWA has replied.
- Label gifted products (#gifted or Paid partnership) and ask collaborators to do the same.
- **Giveaways:** not before 100 real followers. When you run one: a real prize, simple rules, no purchase needed, a clear end date, and the standard "not sponsored by Instagram" line.
