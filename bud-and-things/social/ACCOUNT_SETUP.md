# Instagram account setup: from an empty business account to ready (about 90 minutes, one sitting)

You already have a business account and nothing else. Do these in order. Tick each box. Assets referred to are in `account-assets/`.

## Step 1 · Handle and name (10 min)

- [ ] **Handle.** Send it to me and I'll put it into `brand.json` once, so every graphic picks it up. The website currently links to `@budandthings_offical` (spelled "offical"). If that is your real handle, keep it. If it is a typo, change it now, before you have followers: Instagram allows changing a username, but old links and any printed material break. Whatever you choose, make sure the website footer link and Pinterest/YouTube bios match (the site setting is in the Owner view; one place in the code is fixed text, which I can change after launch).
  Good handle rules: short, no underscores or numbers if you can avoid them, easy to say aloud (`budandthings` is ideal).
- [ ] **Name field** (this is searchable, up to 64 characters; it is not your handle):

### NAME-1
```text
BUD & THINGS by Ashi | Candles & Gifts
```

## Step 3 · Bio (5 min)

Instagram allows **150 characters**. Pick one. Do not put a phone number, address or building name.

### BIO-1 (recommended)

```text
From ₹15 · Wrapped with a note
Made by Ashi, Greater Noida
👇 Shop
```

### BIO-2 (gifting focus)
```text
Small, handmade gifts from ₹15 🎁

```

### BIO-3 (soft, before the shop opens)
```text
Hand-poured candles & Jesmonite objects
Opening soon · join the list 👇
By Ashi · Greater Noida
```

- [ ] **Use BIO-3 until the shop can take orders, then switch to BIO-1.** (A bio that says "Shop" while the site shows Coming soon loses people.)
- [ ] **Category:** choose *Gift Shop* (or *Handmade & Artisan*, whichever Instagram offers).
- [ ] **Link:** `https://budandthings.com`. While Coming soon is on, that page collects emails and gives the welcome code, so the link already does its job.
  Instagram lets you add several links. Second link, once the shop WhatsApp number is set in the Owner view: a WhatsApp chat link to the *shop* number (use `https://wa.me/91<shop number>`; do not use a personal number).
- [ ] **Contact options:** add the shop email only. **Hide phone and address.** Do not add a map or location.
- [ ] Turn on the **Gift Shop / Message** button if offered. Do not turn on a "Directions" button.

## Step 4 · Settings that matter (15 min)

- [ ] **Two-step verification** on (Accounts Center → Password and security). Losing the account loses the business. Add a second person with recovery access (checklist item "two people with recovery access").
- [ ] Switch to a **Professional (Business or Creator) account**: done. Check your category is set.
- [ ] Connect a **Facebook Page** (free) so you can schedule posts in **Meta Business Suite** (this is what lets the weekly 30-minute routine work).
- [ ] **Hidden words** (Settings → Hidden words) on, so rude or spam comments are hidden automatically.
- [ ] **Allow others to share your posts to their Stories:** on. **Allow remixing of Reels:** on. **Tags and mentions:** everyone.
- [ ] **Saved replies** (Business Suite or the Professional tools): create the six in `OUTREACH.md` (price, delivery, sold out, custom, bulk, order help). Add a short **greeting**, not a long auto message.
- [ ] **Alt text:** write a one-line description for each post's photo (also good for search).

## Step 5 · Highlights (30 min)

Seven covers are ready in `account-assets/` (maroon, gold line icon). You need to post a few Stories first, then add them to Highlights. Instagram does not let you create a Highlight from nothing. Post each Story, then **Add to Highlight → New**, name it, and in *Edit cover* upload the image. Names are short so they fit under the circle.

| Highlight name | Cover file | What goes in it (post these as Stories first) |
|---|---|---|
| **Start here** | `highlight-start-here.png` | 4 frames: who you are (face-less is fine: hands + a line); what you make; the price range ("from ₹15"); how to order ("link in bio") |
| **Shop** | `highlight-shop.png` | One frame per best in-stock product, with the link sticker to its page. Replace as stock changes |
| **Gifts** | `highlight-gifts.png` | Gift guide frames (under ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹300), gift wrap and note, gift cards |
| **Making** | `highlight-making.png` | Pour clip, Jesmonite clip, packing clip |
| **Care** | `highlight-care.png` | Three frames: first burn to the edges (about 2 hours), trim the wick to 5 mm, never leave it unattended |
| **FAQ** | `highlight-faq.png` | Delivery time, shipping fee, wrap, note, returns summary (link to the site's policy), "not for eating" on the ladoo candle |
| **Orders** | `highlight-orders.png` | How to order, tracking (the site has Track your order), "your note goes inside" |

## Step 6 · First 9 posts (the grid)

A new profile with 0 posts rarely converts a visitor. Post the nine in `ROLLOUT.md` **before** you message anyone. Nothing is posted yet, so nothing needs deleting.

## Step 7 · Check like a stranger (5 min)

Open your profile on a friend's phone (not logged in). Ask: do I know what this is, what it costs, how to buy, and does it look real?

- [ ] Link works and opens the site
- [ ] Highlights have covers and at least 3 have content
- [ ] No phone number, address, building name or personal name anywhere
- [ ] 9 posts, with real photos of what you ship
