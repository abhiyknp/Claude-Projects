# Photo and clip brief (for the team)

Why: the catalogue pictures are marked **temporary** and look rendered. Posting them as photos of the real pieces could mislead customers, so every graphic, Reel and Story needs real photos and clips. Send this page to whoever is shooting.

**Deadline suggestion:** Tier A (below) by **Sat 21 Nov**, Tier B by **Wed 25 Nov**. Tier A alone is enough to post the first 9 grid posts.

## Rules for every shot

1. **Only what really ships.** Same colour, same size, same finish. No filters, no colour edits, no AI or stock backgrounds. If a prop is in the picture that is not included (a plant, a tray, a book), that is fine, but do not use it in a way that suggests it comes with the product.
2. **Check stock first.** Shoot pieces that are in stock in the Owner view. Do **not** shoot BlueBerry Swirl, Strawberry Cream or Cocoa Bites until they are poured again.
3. **Natural window light**, to one side, no flash, no ring light. Daytime, not at dusk. Steady phone (tripod or a stack of books).
4. **One background:** plain cream, linen or light wood. Wipe glass and trays; trim the wick to about 5 mm; remove fingerprints, dust and wax drips.
5. **Nothing private in frame:** no address, phone number or labels on parcels, no society or building name, no vehicle number plates, no view of the building through the window. Only a hand-written note with **no real name or address** on it.
6. **People:** hands are fine. A face only with the person's yes.
7. **Safety:** lit candles on a heatproof surface, away from fabric and flowers, never left alone.

## Photo specs

- Phone is fine. Shoot **vertical**, 3:4 or 4:5, highest quality. At least **1600 px on the long side**. JPG, under 1.5 MB.
- Keep the product in the centre 70% of the frame (space is needed for text on Stories).
- Shoot each product **three ways:** (a) **hero**, straight on or slightly above, centred; (b) **lit**, close, with the flame in focus (candles only); (c) **in the hand**, to show size.
- **File name = the product's SKU in lower case.** Example: `bnt-prd-cg-002.jpg` for Vanilla Gold (hero). Extra views: add a suffix, `bnt-prd-cg-002-lit.jpg`, `bnt-prd-cg-002-hand.jpg`. The graphics tool picks up the plain SKU name automatically.
