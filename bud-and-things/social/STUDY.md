# BUD & THINGS by Ashi: full study (what the project tells us)

Prepared 18 Nov 2026 from the whole repository: README, site code and copy, catalogue and sheet, launch checklist, backlog, the 28-part audit, the brand kit and the governance docs. **Evidence labels:** *Repo* = read from the project files. *Default* = a site default that the Owner view may have changed, so check it. *Research* = a web source, not verified. *Judgement* = my reasoning.

## 1. What the business is

- **Brand:** BUD & THINGS, display name **By Ashi**. Silk theme: maroon `#8F1834`, gold `#D6A34A`, cream `#F8F0E4`, ink `#150206`; Playfair Display and Montserrat. Tagline: *Made with thought. Given with feeling.* (Repo)
- **What it sells:** hand-poured candles and Jesmonite objects, in small batches, home-based in Greater Noida. Planned future: other objects and fragrances. (Repo)
- **How it sells:** its own website with Razorpay payments and Shiprocket delivery. No marketplace yet. Pinterest product feed is built. (Repo)
- **Tone the site already uses:** warm, short, specific ("Small gift. Real feeling."), with a hand-written note and gift wrap as part of the offer. (Repo)
- **Offers on the site:** welcome code HELLO10 (10% off first order), Bud Points (5% back), gift cards (₹500 / ₹1,000 / ₹2,000), bulk and corporate gifts page, Coming soon page that collects emails. (Repo, Default)

## 2. Readiness: why this affects social media

The launch checklist (updated 16 Nov) still says **NOT READY** until the owner completes items 1 to 7 and one real test order passes: policy details, UPI and WhatsApp settings, online payment on, licence enquiry letters, Shiprocket test booking, **real product photos**, printed labels. (Repo)

Consequences for Instagram:
1. **Posting "we're open" before the shop can take an order would send people to a Coming soon page.** The plan treats 20 Nov as a target, not a promise. If the checklist is not done, Instagram runs "opening soon" content and collects the waitlist (see ROLLOUT.md).
2. **There are no real photos yet.** The catalogue pictures are marked temporary and look rendered. Real photos are the biggest single quality lever, and they are also an advertising-honesty issue.
3. **No burn tests, no IFRA or fragrance safety data, no wax or wick spec** are in the project. So no content may state burn time, wax type, "clean", "natural" or safety claims. (Repo: audit 18, checklist "strongly advised")
4. **Home-based in a residential society.** The checklist says the society RWA's reply "matters most: many societies bar commercial activity or stock". So: **never show the address or building; do not post the shop in society WhatsApp groups until the RWA has replied; avoid "visit us" or pickup.** The audit brief also holds the proprietor's legal name, address and phone numbers. None of those belong on social media. The public identity is **By Ashi** only. (Repo)

### The biggest conversion problem: shipping versus basket size (Default)

Defaults: shipping **₹99**, free over **₹1,999**, gift wrap **₹99**. A ₹15 tealight or ₹29 ladoo candle costs a third to a seventh as much as shipping it. Free shipping at ₹1,999 is more than half the whole catalogue's value. Gift cards start at ₹500.

This is the likeliest reason a follower would like a post and not buy. It is the owner's decision (Owner view, Settings), not a content task, but the strongest levers are:
1. **Bundles as a product:** a "₹499 Secret Santa set" (for example 3 tealights, a dish and a mini candle) with free shipping at about ₹499. Pour them as sets so stock is real.
2. **Lower the free-shipping line** to about ₹499 to ₹699 for the launch season.
3. **Local first orders:** Greater Noida West and Noida are close. Check whether the courier or a delivery partner makes ₹99 smaller for nearby orders. Do not offer pickup (society rule).
4. **Say it upfront** in captions ("shipping ₹99, free over ₹X") so there is no checkout surprise. Hidden costs are a named dark pattern under Indian guidelines.

Whatever is decided, update the captions that quote the shipping fee (`1130-IG` and the FAQ highlight).

## 4. Positioning: the gap to own

Indian candle brands that came up in research include Pinenlime, Umbrtree and Rad Living (named in a YourStory feature; I did not check their accounts or numbers). Many sit at ₹500 to ₹2,000 or more. (Research)

BUD & THINGS has a different shape (Judgement):
- **"The ₹15 to ₹299 gift."** Almost nobody makes a handmade, beautifully wrapped gift for the price of a coffee. This is wide open for Secret Santa, return gifts, colleagues, teachers, hostel and college gifting.
- **Indian warmth, not Scandinavian minimalism.** Mithai, nargis, marigold, ladoo candle, diya-light. A desi gift-shop feeling in maroon and gold.
- **Candle plus object.** Jesmonite trays and dishes make the candle a *gift set*, which most small candle sellers cannot do.
- **Hand-written note, hand-wrapped, ships straight to them.** The experience is part of the product.
- **A real maker.** Ashi pours everything. The making footage is the content competitors cannot copy.

**Positioning line:** *Small, handmade gifts from ₹15, wrapped with a note.*

## 5. Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strengths** | Full brand kit and site already built. Real price ladder with a very low entry point. Making process is photogenic. Gift-wrap, note and bulk-gifting offers exist. Pinterest feed built. Honest-claims culture already in the audit. |
| **Weaknesses** | Tiny stock. No real photos yet. Shipping fee out of scale with basket size. No reviews. Zero Instagram history. Launch checklist not closed. No burn test or safety documents. One person making, packing and posting. |
| **Opportunities** | Secret Santa, Christmas, New Year host gifts, wedding season. Corporate and college gifting. Nano-creator collaborations. Candle and Jesmonite making Reels are watched. Waitlist and welcome code to turn followers into customers. |
| **Threats** | RWA or licence objections. Selling out then looking dormant. Claims that cannot be backed (advertising complaints). Burnout from daily posting. Fire and safety incidents (candles). Copying of designs. |

## 6. What the social plan must do (summary)

1. **Collect the waitlist now** (the Coming soon page already takes emails and gives the HELLO10 welcome code). This makes 20 Nov not the only moment.
2. **Look credible on the first visit:** photo profile picture, clear bio, 7 Highlights, 9 posts before any push.
3. **Turn followers into customers** through the link, WhatsApp enquiries and one clear first-buy offer, not through discounts.
4. **Match attention to stock.** Pour first, post second. Say "next pour" only when true.
5. **Stay inside the honesty and privacy rules** in STRATEGY.md and this study.
