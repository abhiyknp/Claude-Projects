# Festive characters: ready-to-paste prompts

**Current scope: Christmas, New Year, Valentine's Day and Eid only.**

Paste **STYLE + POSE + CHARACTER** as one prompt into ChatGPT images, Gemini or Midjourney. Landscape 3:2. Ask for one image at a time and send each to Claude with its name, for example "penguin, wave" (the look and pose are in the table).

## STYLE (put first, every time)
3D animated-film style character render in Pixar quality: soft glossy materials, fine fur or fabric detail, big expressive eyes, a warm friendly smile, gentle studio lighting, high detail. Plain flat bright green (pure chroma-key green) background with no shadows, gradient or objects on it. Landscape 3:2. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## POSE (put second)
- **PEEK:** The character peeks over a flat horizontal ledge that fills the bottom of the picture. Only the head, shoulders and both hands resting on the edge are visible, looking at the viewer. The ledge is exactly the same flat green as the background. Keep clear space above and around the character.
- **WAVE:** Upper body of the character in the centre of the frame, three-quarter view, waving one hand and smiling at the viewer, the other hand relaxed. No ledge. The body is cropped naturally at the bottom of the frame.

## Tips
- Keep the same look from the PEEK picture when you ask for its WAVE twin: say "the same character as before" or paste the first picture as a reference.
- If the character wears green (an elf, a leaf), tell me when you send it so I keep the green and cut only the background.
- Only send pictures you may use commercially. Avoid real people and brand logos.
- The shop picks characters at random, so two peeking characters per look is enough; more only adds variety.

## What happens after you send them
I cut out the green, add each picture to the shop's list, check it on desktop and phone, and push it for your approval. Nothing else needs doing on your side.
